      From the Ground

      From the Ground explores the interconnectedness of the natural world and our reciprocal relationship with the ecosystems in which we are embedded.

      The exhibition is rooted in the learning principle articulated by the First Nations Education Steering Committee, which emphasizes:

      connectedness, reciprocal relationships, and a sense of place.

      Curated by Holly Schmidt and Amelia Epp, From the Ground brings together drawings, sculptures, prints, and photographs representing the natural world through stories, materials, time, and natural processes.

      Supported by

      • North Vancouver School District
      • North Van Recreation and Culture
      • The Tuey Charitable Foundation
      • The Christopher Foundation

      To learn more about FNESC and the First Peoples Principles of Learning, visit www.fnesc.ca (external site). This sentence carries the inline Poppins, grey and 13.5pt styling pasted into the live page; the prose styles neutralise it.
