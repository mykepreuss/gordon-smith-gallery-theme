**Gordon Smith Gallery Website — Developer Review Notes**

The overall objective is to make the site **more consistent, intuitive and polished at the theme/template level**, while ensuring our internal team can continue populating and updating content without creating visual inconsistencies. 

* **Navigation and dropdown functionality**  
  * Review the main navigation/dropdown behaviour. It currently feels inconsistent and can be difficult to trigger reliably.  
  * Some parent menu items are duplicated inside their own dropdowns, sometimes with different behaviour. Please recommend a cleaner, more intuitive structure.  
  * Establish consistent behaviour for sections that have child pages versus sections that link directly to a page.  
  * The Artists for Kids section includes links to an external website; we'd like the external-link pathway to feel intentional and integrated rather than visually awkward.  
* **Global typography and spacing**  
  * Apply a consistent typography system across navigation, headings, subheadings, body copy and links based on the brand guidelines we will provide.  
  * Review font scale, line spacing, margins, padding, kerning and white space globally rather than page by page.  
  * Some areas appear to be falling back to generic/default typography.  
  * The Gordon Smith Gallery name/identity in the header currently feels visually understated; please review its scale and relationship to the navigation.  
  * We'd like link and CTA styling to feel designed and consistent rather than relying on default treatments such as underlining.  
* **Responsive image behaviour**  
  * Ensure large-format/hero imagery is supported consistently across templates.  
  * Review responsive cropping on desktop, tablet and mobile. At present, images can crop in ways that lose the important part of the image.  
  * Ideally, editors should have some ability to control image focal point/crop where the platform permits.  
  * Where a hero/slideshow pattern is used, establish a consistent position and treatment across page types.  
* **Exhibitions navigation / page architecture**  
  * The current Exhibitions navigation creates some duplication and awkward hierarchy, including parent/child items that repeat one another.  
  * Please review whether the current intermediate landing-page structure is necessary or whether users could move more directly between current, upcoming and past exhibitions.  
  * The Past Exhibitions presentation is working relatively well; the issue is primarily the navigation and pathway into the exhibition content rather than the archive itself.  
  * We will determine final labels and editorial content internally; we're looking for the cleanest technical/structural solution from you.  
* **Shop / Limited Editions**  
  * The main shop/limited-editions landing page currently feels overloaded and repetitive.  
  * The Collections/portfolio navigation near the top appears to provide the necessary pathway into individual portfolios; additional portfolio listings farther down the page may be unnecessary from a UX perspective.  
  * Individual portfolio pages feel considerably cleaner than the main shop landing page. We'd like the landing page streamlined to match that clarity.  
  * Please review the hierarchy of the page and placement of any slideshow/hero component so it follows the broader site system.  
* **Newsletter, contact and social access**  
  * Add or restore a newsletter/Mailchimp signup in a prominent, reusable site-wide location.  
  * Make contact access more persistent/easy to find rather than requiring users to navigate to a dedicated Contact page first.  
  * Provide an appropriate site-wide location for social links/handles.  
  * We will supply the wording, email addresses, signup destination and social URLs; we're asking you to solve the placement and implementation.  
* **Reusable templates/components**  
  * This is probably the most important long-term item: where possible, build stronger reusable components or page templates so staff can populate pages without having to make individual design decisions each time.  
  * Typography, spacing, image proportions, buttons/CTAs, cards, galleries and similar elements should inherit consistent styling automatically.  
  * The goal is for the site to remain visually coherent even as different staff members add and update content over time.  
  * Please flag any areas where the current platform/theme creates limitations that would require custom code or a different structural approach.
